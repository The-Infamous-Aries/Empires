# Messaging Guide

**Welcome to Empire.2 Messaging!** This guide explains how to chat with other players, send private messages, create group rooms, and use the floating chat bubble — all from any page in the game.

---

## Opening the Chat Bubble

The **Chat Bubble** is a floating overlay that stays on screen no matter which page you're on. You don't need to be on a "Messages" tab to use it.

### How to Open/Close

- Click the **💬 button** at the bottom-right corner of the screen
- Press **ESC** to close the window
- Click the button again to reopen

Your open/closed state and last conversation are remembered, even if you refresh the page.

---

## Sending a Message

1. Open the chat bubble
2. Select a conversation from the list (or world/alliance chat)
3. Type your message in the input box at the bottom
4. Press **Enter** or click the **▶ Send** button

Your message appears immediately in the conversation. Other players see it in real-time.

---

## Private Messaging (1-1)

Send a direct message to any other player.

### Starting a Private Conversation

- Select a player from your conversation list, or
- The system auto-creates a private conversation when you message someone for the first time

Private conversations are between you and one other person. No one else can see them.

### Message Rules

- **Cannot message yourself**
- **Blocked users** cannot message each other (in either direction)
- Messages are limited to **2,000 characters**
- You can **edit** your own messages within 15 minutes of sending
- You can **delete** your own messages at any time

---

## Group Rooms (Group Chats)

Create named group rooms for friends, allies, or anyone you want to chat with together.

### Creating a Room

1. Open the chat bubble
2. Click the **+** button in the header
3. Enter a room name (max 100 characters)
4. Click **Create**

The room is created with you as the creator. You're the only member initially.

### Inviting Others

1. Open the group room
2. Click **Invite** (or use the room menu)
3. Select a player to invite

Only room participants can invite others. Rooms have a maximum of 100 members by default.

### Room Controls

| Action | Who Can Do It | Description |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Rename room | Creator only | Change the room display name |
| Archive room | Any participant | Hide the room from your list (unarchive anytime) |
| Mute room | Any participant | Stop notifications from this room |
| Kick member | Creator only | Remove a participant from the room |
| Delete room | Creator only | Permanently delete the room and all its messages |

### Leaving a Room

You can leave any group room at any time. If you're the last person to leave and you're not the creator, the empty room is cleaned up automatically. If the creator leaves, the room is deleted entirely.

## World Chat & Alliance Chat

### World Chat

- **Global channel** — every player can read and write
- Good for announcements, questions, and general conversation
- Message history keeps the latest 1,000 messages

### Alliance Chat

- **Alliance members only** — you must be in an alliance to use it
- Private to your alliance
- Message history keeps the latest 500 messages

## Sending Tips (Chat Money)

You can send money to another player through chat.

### How to Tip

1. Open a private conversation with the player
2. Use the tip command (or tip button in the UI)
3. Enter the amount and an optional message
4. Confirm the transfer

**Rules:**
- Minimum tip: **$1**
- You must have enough money in your nation
- You cannot tip yourself
- The recipient receives the money instantly
- A tip message appears in your private conversation

---

## Blocking Users

If someone is bothering you, you can block them.

- **Blocked users cannot send you messages**
- **You cannot send messages to blocked users**
- Blocking works in both directions — if they blocked you, you can't message them either
- Unblock anytime to restore messaging

---

## Typing Indicators

When someone is typing in a conversation, you'll see **"Name is typing…"** below the conversation list. This works in real-time across all conversation types.

---

## Unread Messages

- **Unread badge** on the chat bubble button shows your total unread count
- **Unread badges** appear next to individual conversations in the list
- Selecting a conversation automatically marks its messages as read
- Badges clear when you open the conversation

---

## Cross-Tab Support

If you have Empire.2 open in multiple browser tabs:
- Messages sent in one tab appear in all other tabs
- Your chat bubble state (open/closed) syncs across tabs
- You won't miss messages just because you switched tabs

---

## Message History

Each conversation type keeps a limited history:

| Conversation | Max Messages |
|--------------|--------------|
| World Chat | 1,000 |
| Alliance Chat | 500 |
| Private Messages | 500 |
| Group Rooms | 300 |

Older messages are automatically removed to save memory.

## Tips & Strategy

### Early Game
- Open the chat bubble and say hello in World Chat
- Join or create an alliance to access Alliance Chat
- Start private conversations with nearby players to coordinate

### Mid Game
- Create a group room for your alliance officers or trading partners
- Use tips to send money to allies or trade partners
- Block spammers or toxic players immediately

### Late Game
- Use group rooms for complex coordination (wars, treaties, large trades)
- Keep your conversation list tidy by archiving rooms you're done with
- Mute noisy rooms you still want to be in

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to be on the Messages page to chat?**
A: No! The chat bubble works from any page in the game.

**Q: Can I message someone who isn't online?**
A: Yes. Messages are stored and they'll see them when they open their chat bubble.

**Q: Can I delete messages I sent?**
A: Yes, you can delete your own messages at any time. You cannot delete other people's messages.

**Q: Can I edit messages?**
A: Yes, within 15 minutes of sending. After that, the message is locked.

**Q: What happens if I leave a group room?**
A: You're removed from the participant list. If you're the last person to leave, the room is cleaned up. If the creator leaves, the room is deleted.

**Q: Can I rename a group room I didn't create?**
A: No, only the room creator can rename it.

**Q: Is there a limit to group room size?**
A: Yes, 100 members maximum by default.

**Q: Are messages saved if I refresh the page?**
A: Yes, conversation history is kept in memory during your session, and messages are synced to the server for persistence across sessions.
