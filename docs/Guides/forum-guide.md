## What Is the Forum

The Forum is a player-driven discussion board built into the game. You can:

- Create posts in different categories (news, strategy, roleplay, announcements, and more)
- Comment on posts and reply to other comments
- React to posts and comments with ratings
- Send money tips to other players
- Report content you find harmful or inappropriate
- Receive notifications when someone tips you or reviews your report

There is **no profanity filter** — players have full free speech. However, other players can report content they find bad or harmful, and moderators will review those reports.

---

## Creating a Post

To create a post, you need:

- **Title** — up to 200 characters
- **Content** — up to 10,000 characters
- **Category** — choose from the available categories (defaults to General Discussion)
- **Media (optional)** — up to 5 images (JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP, max 10 MB each)
- **Tags (optional)** — keywords to help others find your post

### Rate Limit

You can create up to **10 posts per hour**. If you hit the limit, you'll need to wait before posting again.

## Commenting and Replies

You can comment on any post that isn't locked. Comments support:

- **Nested replies** — Reply to a specific comment to create a conversation thread
- **Media** — Up to 3 images per comment
- **Editing** — Edit your own comments (moderators can edit any comment)
- **Deletion** — Delete your own comments (moderators can delete any comment)

### Rate Limit

You can create up to **30 comments per hour**.

### Locked Posts

Moderators can lock posts to prevent new comments. You won't be able to comment on a locked post.

## Reaction Ranking

The forum uses a unique reaction system with **4 types** and a **1–6 rating scale**:

| Type | Group | Icon | Use For |
|------|-------|------|---------|
| **Like** | Sentiment | `like-X.png` | You like the content |
| **Dislike** | Sentiment | `dislike-X.png` | You dislike the content |
| **Lol** | Reaction | `lol-X.png` | The content is funny |
| **Wtf** | Reaction | `wtf-X.png` | The content is surprising |

### How It Works

- **Rating:** Choose a rating from **1 to 6** for each reaction type.
- **One per group:** You can pick only one Sentiment reaction (Like OR Dislike) and only one Reaction (Lol OR WTF) per post or comment.
- **Mix and match:** You can combine one Sentiment + one Reaction (for example, Like + Lol, or Dislike + WTF).
- **Change your mind:** Reacting again in the same group replaces your previous reaction and rating.

**Examples of valid combinations:**
- Like (5) + Lol (3)
- Dislike (2) + WTF (6)
- Like (4) only
- No reactions at all

**Examples of invalid combinations:**
- Like (5) + Dislike (3) — same group, not allowed
- Lol (4) + WTF (2) — same group, not allowed

## Tipping Other Nations

You can send money to other players through the forum as a tip. This is a great way to reward good content, show appreciation, or support allies.

### How to Tip

1. Choose the recipient nation
2. Enter an amount (minimum **$1**)
3. Optionally attach the tip to a specific post or comment
4. Add an optional message

### Rules

- You **cannot tip yourself**
- You must have sufficient funds
- The recipient must exist
- Tips are transferred through the game's economy system
- You can send up to **20 tips per hour**
- The recipient receives a notification

## Moderation and Reports

### Reports (No Profanity Filter)

The forum has **no profanity filter** — players have full free speech. Instead, the community polices itself through reports.

If you see a post or comment you find bad or harmful, you can report it. Choose a reason:

| Reason | When to Use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Spam** | Repeated or irrelevant content |
| **Harassment** | Targeted abuse or hostility toward a player |
| **Harmful Content** | Content that may cause harm |
| **Misinformation** | False or misleading claims |
| **Other** | Any other reason not listed above |

You can file up to **10 reports per hour**.

### What Happens After You Report

1. Your report is created with status **Pending**
2. All moderators receive a notification
3. A moderator reviews your report
4. The moderator sets the status to **Reviewed**, **Dismissed**, or **Actioned**
5. You receive a notification when your report is reviewed

### Moderator Actions

Moderators can:
- Pin posts to the top of a category
- Lock posts to prevent new comments
- Hide posts from regular users (content is still accessible to moderators)
- Edit or delete any post or comment
- Review and act on reports

---

## Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:

- **Tips** — When someone sends you money
- **Reports** (moderators only) — When a new report is filed
- **Report reviews** — When a report you filed is reviewed

You can view your notifications, see which ones are unread, and mark them as read individually or all at once.
