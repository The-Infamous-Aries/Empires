# Research & Technology Guide

**Welcome to the Research System!** This guide explains how research works in Empire.2 — from laboratories and funding to tech levels, war skills, healthcare, and economy upgrades.

---

## How Research Works

Research is **goal-based, not time-based**. There are no timers, hours, or days to wait. Instead:

1. **Pick a research project** you want to study
2. **Build Laboratories** — they divert a percentage of your turn income into research points
3. **Watch the progress bar** fill up as points accumulate
4. **When the goal is reached**, research completes automatically and you can pick another project

### No Time, No Upfront Cost

- **No time-based costs** — research doesn't take "X hours" to complete
- **No upfront money cost** — you don't pay to start research
- **Funded per-turn** — laboratories divert a portion of your tax income every turn
- **Auto-completes** — once the goal is met, research finishes on its own

---

## Laboratories: Your Research Engine

Research Laboratories are the ONLY way to fund research. Without at least one lab, research points cannot be generated.

### How Labs Work

| Labs Built | Income Diverted | Research Points per Turn |
|------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 0 | 0% | 0 (no research possible) |
| 1 | 2.5% | Small amount |
| 2 | 5% | More points |
| 3 | 7.5% | Even more |
| 4 (max) | 10% | Maximum base rate |

### Lab Penalties

Each laboratory also generates pollution and disease:

| Labs | Pollution | Disease |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | +5 | +5 |
| 4 | +20 | +20 |

### Advanced Research Institute (Project)

Building this project (max 1) improves your labs:

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| 2.5% income per lab | 3.5% income per lab |
| 10% total (4 labs) | 14% total (4 labs) |
| +5 pollution/disease per lab | +6 pollution/disease per lab |

### Wonders That Affect Labs

| Wonder | Requires | Effect |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Grand Research Academy** | Advanced Research Institute | +1.5% income per lab (+2 pollution/disease per lab) |
| **Environmental Research Complex** | — | Cuts lab pollution & disease by 50% |

## Starting & Managing Research

### Starting Research

1. Open the Research Panel
2. Choose a category (Economy, Healthcare, or Military)
3. Select a research project you qualify for (tech level + prerequisites met)
4. Click **Start Research**

### Research Rules

- **One project at a time** (Great Library wonder adds a second slot)
- **Tech level required** — your nation's tech level must meet or exceed the research's tier
- **Prerequisites** — some research requires earlier projects to be completed first
- **No duplicates** — you can't research the same thing twice

### Canceling Research

You can cancel an active project at any time. Progress is NOT lost — if you restart the same project later, it continues from where it left off.

## The Three Research Branches

### 💰 Economy — Financial Systems & Trade

Economy research improves your nation's income, costs, and efficiency.

**What it affects:**
- **Taxable Income** — increases how much income your population generates
- **Infrastructure Costs** — reduces the price to buy buildings and land
- **Infrastructure Upkeep** — reduces the daily cost to maintain buildings
- **Crime** — reduces crime rate in your cities
- **Pollution** — reduces pollution levels
- **Environment** — improves environmental score
- **Happiness** — increases citizen happiness
- **Population Growth** — increases population growth rate
- **Seniors Working** (later levels) — allows seniors to work and be taxed at half rate

**Cost Range:** $10M (Tier 1) to $10T (Tier 12)

---

### 🏥 Healthcare — Medical Knowledge & Public Health

Healthcare research fights disease and improves your citizens' well-being.

**What it affects:**
- **Disease Spawn Rate** — reduces how often new diseases appear
- **Disease Level** — reduces the severity of existing diseases
- **Cure Cost** — reduces the money cost to cure diseases
- **Pollution** — reduces pollution from medical facilities
- **Population Growth** — increases growth rate (stacks with religion bonuses)
- **Energy Skill** (player skill) — increases action capacity
- **Intuition Skill** (player skill) — improves various success rates

**Cost Range:** $10M (Tier 1) to $10T (Tier 12)

---

### ⚔️ Military — Technology, Tactics & Warfare

War research boosts your military capabilities and unit performance.

**What it affects (Player Skills):**
- **Attack** — increases your attack power in combat
- **Defense** — increases your defense capability
- **Aim** — improves accuracy and ranged attacks
- **Luck** — improves luck-based outcomes
- **Crit Chance** — increases critical hit probability
- **Crit Damage** — increases critical hit damage
- **Block** — improves block chance
- **Parry** — improves parry chance

**What it affects (Units):**
- **Soldier/Tank/Aircraft/Ship Cost** — reduces purchase cost
- **Soldier/Tank/Aircraft/Ship Upkeep** — reduces daily maintenance cost
- **Soldier/Tank/Aircraft/Ship Effectiveness** — increases combat performance

**What it affects (Weapons):**
- **Missile Damage** — increases missile strike damage
- **Nuke Damage** — increases nuclear weapon damage

**Cost Range:** $10M (Tier 1) to $10T (Tier 12)

## Boost Limits by Tier

Research projects have a limited number of effects based on their tier:

| Tier Range | Max Effects Per Research |
|------------|-------------------------|
| Tiers 1–5 | 1 effect per research |
| Tiers 6–12 | 2 effects per research |

This means early-game research is focused (one benefit at a time), while late-game research can provide multiple benefits simultaneously.

## Strategy Tips

### Early Game (Tiers 1–3)
- Build your first Laboratory as soon as possible
- Focus on 1-research projects (tier limit)
- Prioritize economy research to boost income for more labs
- Build healthcare research to reduce disease early

### Mid Game (Tiers 4–7)
- Build up to 4 laboratories for maximum income diversion
- Consider the Advanced Research Institute project for 3.5% lab rate
- Start military research to protect your nation
- Build the Great Library wonder for a second research slot

### Late Game (Tiers 8–12)
- Research costs become very high ($1B+)
- Consider the Grand Research Academy (+1.5% per lab) and Environmental Complex (−50% pollution/disease)
- Focus on high-impact 2-effect research (tier 6+)
- Nuclear weapons, quantum finance, and other tier 11–12 techs are game-changers

## Technology Entity

The **Technology** entity (`entities/Technology.js`) wraps research data and integrates with the Research System. It provides:

- **Data loading** — pulls research info from data files
- **Start/cancel research** — delegates to ResearchSystem
- **Progress tracking** — shows completion percentage
- **Effect application** — applies completed research effects to your nation
- **Collaborative research** — bonuses when multiple nations research together
- **Serialization** — save/load support for game state

### Common Technology Effects

| Effect | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| `taxable_income_boost` | More income from your population |
| `population_growth_bonus` | Faster population growth |
| `population_boost` | Instant population increase |
| `infra_upkeep_reduction` | Cheaper building maintenance |
| `infra_cost_reduction` | Cheaper building purchases |
| `land_cost_reduction` | Cheaper land purchases |
| `pollution_reduction` | Less pollution |
| `crime_reduction` | Less crime |
| `happiness_bonus` | Happier citizens |
| `environment_bonus` | Better environment score |
| `cure_cost_reduction` | Cheaper disease cures |
| `disease_level_reduction` | Less severe diseases |
| `seniors_work_tax_half` | Seniors contribute half-rate taxes |
| `energy_skill_bonus` | More action energy (player skill) |
| `intuition_skill_bonus` | Better intuition (player skill) |
