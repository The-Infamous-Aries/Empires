## Government System

Your government determines how your nation is run. Each government type provides **3 boosts** and **2 penalties** that affect real game mechanics such as tax collection, military strength, population efficiency, pollution, disease, crime, and building upkeep.

No government is strictly "better" than another — each excels in different areas and has weaknesses to balance them. Your choice should match your nation's strategy and your playstyle.

### Liberal Democracy 🗳️
**Liberal Democratic Republic**

A system governed by elected representatives with strong emphasis on individual rights, free markets, and constitutional limits on government power.

**Boosts:**
- Taxable income +10%
- Environment +10%
- Happiness +10%

**Penalties:**
- Military strength -10%
- Military morale -10%

Best for: Economic growth, happy citizens, clean environment. Not ideal for aggressive military expansion.

---

### Constitutional Monarchy 👑
**Constitutional Monarchy**

A balanced system where a hereditary monarch serves as head of state within a constitutional framework. Power is shared between the crown and an elected parliament.

**Boosts:**
- Stability +10%
- Environment +5%
- Military strength +5%

**Penalties:**
- Pollution +10%
- Tax efficiency -8%

Best for: Stable nations with moderate military needs. Watch your pollution levels.

## How Bonuses & Penalties Work

### Good Metrics (higher is better)

These are increased by boosts and decreased by penalties:

- **Happiness** — Citizen satisfaction. Higher happiness improves population efficiency and taxable income.
- **Stability** — How stable your government is. Higher stability reduces unrest.
- **Tax Efficiency** — How effectively taxes are collected. Higher efficiency means more revenue per tax point.
- **Tech Level** — Your technological advancement. Higher tech improves research and military capabilities.
- **Population Efficiency** — How effectively your population produces. Higher efficiency means more output per person.
- **Military Strength** — Your raw military power.
- **Military Morale** — How effective your troops are in combat.
- **Literacy** — Education level of your population. Improves research and economy.
- **Environment** — Environmental quality. Higher is cleaner and healthier.

### Bad Metrics (higher is worse)

These are reduced by boosts and increased by penalties:

- **Pollution** — Industrial waste and environmental damage. Higher pollution reduces happiness and environment.
- **Disease** — Public health issues. Higher disease reduces population and happiness.
- **Crime** — Criminal activity. Higher crime reduces stability and happiness.

### Example Calculation

If your nation has `happiness = 50` and you adopt **Buddhism** (happiness +15%):

```
50 × (1 + 0.15) = 57.5 → 57 happiness
```

If your nation has `pollution = 30` and you adopt **Shintoism** (which has no pollution bonus/penalty), pollution stays at 30.

If your nation adopts **Polytheism** (pollution +10%):

```
30 × (1 + 0.10) = 33 pollution
```

### Stacking with Government

Government and religion bonuses are applied independently and stack together. For example:

- Liberal Democracy (taxable income +10%) + Atheism (tax efficiency +12%) = Both apply to your economy
- Military Junta (military strength +12%) + Monotheism (military morale +10%) = Strong military with high morale

## Changing Your Government or Religion

### Government Changes

Government changes are not instant. They require:

1. **Government Monument** wonder (unlocks government changes)
2. **14-day cooldown** between changes
3. **Government Statue** project (enables the change mechanism)

When you change governments, the new government's bonuses and penalties replace the old ones. Your nation's stats recalculate immediately.

### Religion Changes

Religion changes require:

1. **Religious Monument** wonder (unlocks religion changes)
2. **14-day cooldown** between changes
3. **Religious Statue** project (enables the change mechanism)

When you change religions, the new religion's bonuses and penalties replace the old ones.

### Demand Timers

Your citizens may occasionally demand a government or religion change. These demands appear randomly every 7-30 game days. You can choose to comply or ignore them, but ignoring demands may affect stability and happiness.
